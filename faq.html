Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first order.

Ordering & Store
Is the HIFUN 3 store live yet?+
We're putting the finishing touches on our very first drop. The products you see on the site are a preview of what's coming — full checkout will be live very soon. Follow along or reach out on our Contact page and we'll let you know the moment we launch.
What kind of quality can I expect?+
Heavyweight cotton fabric, reinforced stitching and fits that are actually cut for comfort — not corner-cut to save cost. Quality is the entire reason HIFUN 3 exists, so we don't compromise on it.
What payment methods will be available?+
Once the store goes live, you'll be able to pay via UPI, cards, net banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) — whichever works best for you.
Shipping & Delivery
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?+
Yes. COD will be available across India alongside prepaid options once the store is fully live.
Is shipping free?+
Yes, shipping is free on all prepaid orders across India. A small nominal fee may apply on COD orders.
Do you ship pan-India?+
Yes, once live, HIFUN 3 will ship to every serviceable pin code across India.
How can I track my order?+
After launch, every order will come with a tracking link shared over SMS, email, and WhatsApp so you always know where your package is.
Sizing, Returns & Exchange
How do I know which size to pick?+
A detailed size chart will be available on every product page. If you're still unsure, message us directly on WhatsApp and we'll help you pick the right fit.
What's your return or exchange policy?+
If an item doesn't fit right or arrives damaged, you'll be able to request a return or exchange within a set window of delivery, as long as the tags are intact and the item is unworn. Full policy details will be published before launch.
About the Brand
Where is HIFUN 3 based?+
HIFUN 3 is a homegrown Indian brand, built and run by three friends. You can read the full story on our About Us page.
How do I contact HIFUN 3 directly?+
Head over to our Contact page — you can reach any of the three founders directly on Instagram, Facebook, or WhatsApp.

Still have a question?

Message us directly and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
